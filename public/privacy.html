Privacy Policy

Calorie & Macro Tracker is a personal, single-user nutrition and fitness tracking app. It has no user accounts, no server-side database, and no analytics or tracking of any kind. This page explains what data the app touches and where it goes.

Data stored locally in your browser

Your profile (body stats, goal, optional photo), food log, and synced WHOOP metrics are stored only in your browser's localStorage. None of it is sent to any server the app operator controls. Clearing your browser data or using a different device/browser starts fresh.

Third-party services this app talks to

  • Open Food Facts — barcode and food-name lookups are sent to Open Food Facts's public API to retrieve nutrition facts. No account information or personal data is included in these requests, only the barcode or search text.
  • WHOOP — if you choose to connect WHOOP, you authorize this app via WHOOP's own OAuth login (you never share your WHOOP password with this app). The app requests read-only access to your physiological cycles and recovery data (scopes: read:cycles, read:recovery, offline) to display calories burned, strain, and recovery score alongside your food log. WHOOP access and refresh tokens are stored in your browser's localStorage. The OAuth token exchange is proxied through a small Cloudflare Worker operated by the app owner, whose only job is holding the WHOOP client secret required by WHOOP's OAuth flow — it does not store or log your WHOOP data.

What this app does not do

  • No ads, no analytics, no third-party trackers.
  • No data is sold or shared with anyone beyond the two services above.
  • No account creation or password of any kind for this app itself.

Revoking access

You can disconnect WHOOP at any time from within the app, which clears the stored tokens from your browser. You can also revoke this app's access directly from your WHOOP account settings at any time.

Contact

This is an independently operated personal project, not a commercial product. If you have questions about this policy, contact the app owner directly.
